# Find a specific photo

People remember a photo through a mix of confident facts ("Dad was in it"),
guesses ("sometime last spring"), and content ("holding a big fish"). Put
confident facts into filters, give guesses wide ranges, and describe the
content as a short visual phrase ("man holding a large fish on a boat")
rather than the user's whole sentence.

When the photo does not turn up, relax the clue the user was least sure of
first. A face may also be unrecognized or unassigned, and many photos have no
location, so person and place filters can hide the photo even when the user
is right. Changing one clue at a time shows which one was wrong; change
several at once when the evidence points that way.

For a narrow time window, skip search and browse everything from those hours
in order.

If you cannot find it, tell the user which clues you tried so they can
correct the one that was wrong.
